
Tetris has a few fundamental components:

1. Board: The rectangular area where blocks are placed.
2. Pieces: Shapes made up of smaller square blocks.
3. Movement: Pieces can move left, right and down.
4. Rotation: Pieces can rotate into different orientations.
5. Collision detection: The game checks whether a movement is allowed.
6. Locking: When a piece cannot move further down, it becomes part of the board.
7. Line clearing: Complete horizontal rows are removed.

Game has the following loop:
Create a new piece;
Read player input;
Check whether movement is possible
Move the piece
If it cannot move down, store it on the board
Repeat until the game ends
